About The Project
This platform is an interactive web-based educational game that adapts the concept of a virtual escape room for chemistry learning. The system is designed to be linear and rigorous to measure student understanding. Students cannot break into the room until they complete a pretest. Inside the room, they must solve various chemistry puzzles to obtain the key code to escape. This concludes with a posttest to measure their final academic achievement.
