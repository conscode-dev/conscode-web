About The Project
This system is a web/mobile-based employee attendance management platform designed to replace manual processes with a high-performance, automated ecosystem. Prioritizing a clean user experience (The Soul) and a robust tracking engine (The Engine), this system ensures that all attendance data is recorded accurately, transparently, and anti-manipulation.
