About The Project
This platform is a web-based learning system that transforms educational materials into a fantasy web comic series. Through a visual literacy approach, users not only consume stories as readers (Material & Dictionary) but are also encouraged to become story producers (Creative Space), while also testing their understanding through interactive challenges (Evaluation), with all performance monitored in real time by teachers through a ranking control center (Admin Page).
